# Suppliers

<!-- block zr6rbl -->

##### Information for Suppliers

We welcome new partnerships with reliable suppliers of quality products. You can find all the information below which you need to apply to be a supplier here.

#### Mill Vetting & Procurement Framework

Our partner mills are selected based on strict metallurgical capabilities and global compliance frameworks.

🏭

##### Accredited Tier-1 Status

Mills must hold active ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and IATF 16949 (Automotive Quality) structural certifications.

Mandatory

🔍

##### Full Metallurgical Tracing

Continuous data logs logging direct furnace heats, raw input scrap ratios, and continuous casting timelines.

Mandatory

🌡️

##### Advanced Finishing Assets

In-house capability for Spheroidize Annealing (+AC), Quenching & Tempering (+QT), and Centerless Precision Grinding.

Mandatory

**Quality** – Excellent product quality  
**Cost** – Competitive prices and continuous cost optimization  
**Delivery** – Reliable and flexible delivery  
**Service** – Customer focus  
**Sustainability** – Social and ecological sustainability (CSR)
