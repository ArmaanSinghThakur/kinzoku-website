In the European timber, packaging, and construction sectors, fastener selection directly impacts operational throughput, structural safety, and regulatory compliance. Whether feeding high-speed automated pallet lines (such as CAPE, Storti, or Gulliver) or securing timber frame structures, specifying correct mechanical properties, wire tensile strength, and coatings is vital to preventing equipment downtime and load rejection.

## 1\. EPAL-Certified Pallet Nails (UIC 435-2 Standards)

The European Pallet Association (EPAL) strictly controls fastener specifications to ensure Euro pallets (EPAL 1, EPAL 2, EPAL 3) withstand heavy dynamic forklift handling across international logistics networks. Non-certified nails risk load failure and pallet rejection across EU logistics hubs.

| Standard Size | Application Area | Shank Type | Key Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2.80 x 40mm** | Pallet Repair & Deck Boards | Ring Shank | High withdrawal resistance |
| **3.50/3.70 x 70mm** | Board-to-Block Assembly | Threaded / Ring Shank | High shear & bending strength |
| **3.50/3.70 x 90mm** | Corner Block Fastening | Annular Ring Shank | Extreme pull-out strength |

**Quality Guarantee:** Every EPAL nail features official manufacturer head stamping, high tensile wire drawing to prevent head shearing under high pneumatic pressure, and strict shank alignment for zero jam rates on automated lines.

## 2\. Collated Wire Coil Nails & Industrial Staples

#### 15° – 16° Wire-Collated Coil Nails

Diameter: 2.1mm – 3.8mm | Length: 25mm – 100mm | Angle: 15° – 16°

Engineered for high-volume packaging, crate assembly, fencing, pallet production, and timber construction across Europe.

**100% Tool & Machine Compatibility:**

Guaranteed seamless feeding in Europe’s most widely used pneumatic tools (**Stanley Bostitch, MAX Europe, Paslode, BeA, Haubold, Senco, Prebena, Omer, Tjep, Holz-Her, Alsafix, Kihlberg**) and automated lines (**CAPE, Storti**).

**Key Performance Advantages:**

-   **Zero Jamming:** Precision weld-wire collation eliminates misfires on manual and robotic lines.
-   **No Bending or Shearing:** High-tensile steel wire drives easily through dense hardwoods and frozen timber.
-   **Maximized Uptime:** Standard (225–300) and Jumbo coils (up to 1,000 nails) reduce reload stops.
-   **3 Shank Options:** _Screw_ (max hold in softwoods), _Ring_ (extreme pull-out resistance / EN 14592), _Smooth_ (fast assembly).
-   **4 Coating Grades:** Bright, Electro-Galvanized, Hot-Dip Galvanized (>50µm), and Stainless Steel (A2/A4).

#### Heavy-Duty Industrial Staples

14G, 16G (N-Series) & 32/35 Carton Series

Designed for heavy timber framing, crate manufacturing, pallet collar assembly, and carton closing. Fully compatible with BeA, Prebena, Senco, Bostitch, and Omer heavy-duty staplers. High-tensile galvanized steel wire eliminates leg deflection in hardwoods.

## 3\. Loose Bulk Common Nails (EN 10230-1)

Loose common nails remain an essential material for European carpentry, temporary site formwork, and heavy wooden crate packaging.

-   **Dimensions:** Wire diameters from 2.2 mm to 6.0 mm; lengths from 40 mm to 200 mm (Popular EU sizes: 2.8×65mm, 3.1×80mm, 3.8×100mm, 4.2×120mm).
-   **Head & Point:** Flat countersunk grid head to prevent hammer slippage with sharp diamond points for split-free wood entry.
-   **Bulk Packaging:** Sturdy 20 kg or 25 kg corrugated cartons stacked on 1,000 kg Euro pallets, or custom 2.5 kg – 5 kg branded distributor boxes.
