## Machine-Grade Quality That Protects Tooling

Running nail manufacturing lines or wire drawing machinery requires consistent metallurgy. The wrong wire causes feed slippage, die wear, and costly downtime. We deliver engineered low carbon wire tailored to your production equipment:

-   **Enkotec Rotary Systems:** Bright, mechanically clean surface for dry oil-free processing.
-   **Wafios Linear Lines:** Uniform tensile strength to eliminate hard spots and protect carbide pins.
-   **Vitari & Eurolls Straighteners:** Strict ASTM A510M ovality tolerances for zero slippage.
-   **Hassle-Free EU Imports:** CBAM data and EN 10204 3.1 mill certificates.
