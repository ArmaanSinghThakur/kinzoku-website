## Our Model

Kinzoku Consultancy & Trade operates as a **steel trading company** with deep sourcing capabilities across Asia. We purchase directly from mills and deliver to European buyers at competitive prices — fully documented, compliant, and transparent.

**Step 1:** **You request.** Send us your specifications, grade, quantity, and delivery timeline.

**Step 2:** **We source.** We identify and verify mills in India, Japan, China, and Vietnam capable of production.

**Step 3:** **We quote.** We negotiate mill-direct pricing and provide you with a transparent, all-inclusive quote.

**Step 4:** **You confirm.** You approve the order and pricing.

**Step 5:** **We manage.** Production oversight, quality verification, documentation, logistics.

**Step 6:** **You receive.** Goods delivered to your warehouse, fully documented, with one EUR invoice.

## Delivery Options

**EXW** — Ex-works at mill gate. Best for buyers with own logistics.

**FOB** — Free on board, port of export. Best for buyers with freight forwarders.

**CIF** — Cost, insurance, freight to port. Best for buyers who clear customs themselves.

**DDP** — Delivered duty paid to your warehouse. Best for buyers who want zero import complexity.

Note: Full DDP with Kinzoku as importer of record requires CBAM declarant authorization. For buyers with their own authorization, we coordinate delivery to your specified location.
